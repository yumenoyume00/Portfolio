ABOUT ME

I studied Environmental Design in China before becoming more interested in visual communication. Right now, I am studying Visual Design at Kuwasawa in Tokyo, working across UI/UX, branding, editorial and graphic design.

I’m an outgoing ENTP who enjoys talking with people and stepping into unfamiliar environments. During university in China, I played bass in a band and spent several years doing debate. Since coming to Japan to study, I have also worked as an exhibition interpreter, taught Japanese and worked at a gallery.

I enjoy learning languages and visiting exhibitions. I also like making collages from posters and flyers I collect. I hope to become a designer who can connect clear visual systems with engaging digital experiences.

This portfolio consists of physical landscape and portrait editions together with this website. The two books organize my visual and UI / UX work separately, while all three formats share one visual system. I would be happy if they are viewed together, including how I selected images and adapted the layouts for each medium.
